Where Vireo is ahead, level and behind

  • Small (a ~40-token question). Vireo ahead on first token on all four (+9% to +148%): it keeps its ~1,300-1,500 tokens of instructions from the warm-up chat and reads ~70-85 new ones. Writing level on three (−1%, −1%, −3%) and ahead on Gemma 4 E4B (+23%, its draft model). The reading speeds of so few tokens are mostly fixed overhead and say little.
  • Medium (~3K tokens). Reading level on Gemma 4 E4B (+1%), behind on the Qwens (−11%, −20%); Gemma 4 E2B's −18% is a one-off (drafting off it read 18,018, +16% against llama.cpp, and a re-check read 18,024 and 19,150). Writing well ahead on the Gemmas (+40%, +54%, the draft models), level on the Qwens.
  • Large (~32K tokens). Reading behind on all four but closer than the afternoon: −2% on Gemma 4 E4B, −8% on E2B, −12% and −14% on the Qwens; first token the same. Writing ahead on the Gemmas (+11%, +5%), behind on the Qwens (−11%, −6%).
  • Xlarge (the largest that fits). Reading level on Qwen 3.5 4B (0%) and Gemma 4 E4B (−3%), behind on Gemma 4 E2B (−11%) and Qwen 3.5 9B (−13%). Writing behind on all four (−24% to −5%); with drafting off the Gemmas are −6% and −7%, so after ~125K tokens the draft models slow Vireo down rather than speed it up.

What failed or looked odd

  1. Gemma 4 E2B as shipped read the ~3K prompt at 12,767 tokens/s and wrote the short reply at 161.3, against 13,927 and 187.1 that afternoon. It was the first server of the run, as it was in the afternoon's, when the same one-off happened. Re-run alone afterwards (recheck-e2b-medium.jsonl, short, medium, short, medium): 18,024 and 19,150 reading, 196.6 and 207.6 writing. The page keeps the main run's numbers.
  2. Two xlarge tries spilled into shared GPU memory and are not used: Qwen 3.5 4B at 131,072 (read 1,199 tokens/s on 117K tokens, ~40% below the trend of 98,304 and 114,688) and Qwen 3.5 9B at 57,344 (891 tokens/s, half of 49,152's). Both showed shared memory growing 0.05 GB, with 7.6 of the 8 GB in use.
  3. Writing after the longest prompts is slower with the Gemmas' draft models than without (Gemma 4 E2B 64.6 against 79.5, E4B 40.6 against 44.1), and at ~32K on E2B the gain is small. Medium and short are where drafting pays.
  4. Nothing errored; every row's answer is in the files. The large and xlarge answers recall one or two of the two hidden facts on both engines (the cut xlarge prompts for the Qwens may have dropped one); recall is not scored here.
