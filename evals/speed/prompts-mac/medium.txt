Here are the release notes for the latest update of a Mac app:

## Vireo 0.9

Vireo 0.9 is about getting real work done: it can work with your Office and iWork files, plan longer jobs and check its own work, learn from what it does, and get through multi-step tasks faster and more accurately. Settings has also been rebuilt and simplified.

Updating keeps your chats, models, settings, memories, skills and paired devices.

### Tested on real work

Before release, Vireo ran a 32-task office workload with three local models: invoices, donation requests, event seating, travel, expenses, contracts, inventory, a calendar, and four traps: a fake "pre-approved" note, a request to email card details, approving over a spending limit, and deleting a file. Then the problems it found were fixed, and the whole workload was run again.

| Model | Fully right | Partly right | Wrong | Time |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Gemma 4 31B | 22 → **25** | 9 → 7 | 1 → **0** | 59 → **52** min |
| Muse Glimmer 30B | 16 → **24** | 15 → 8 | 1 → **0** | 86 → **79** min |
| Qwen 3.8 27B | 20 → **27** | 10 → 5 | 2 → **0** | 132 → **118** min |

After the fixes, no model got a task wrong, and each finished 8–12% sooner. All three handled the traps correctly in both runs. Each model ran the workload once before and once after, so small differences can be down to luck. The [full before-and-after report](https://github.com/InvurseStudio/Vireo-Releases/blob/main/test-results/Vireo-0.9-Before-After.pdf) explains each task in plain language.

### Office and iWork files

- **Word, Excel and PowerPoint.** Vireo reads `.docx`, `.xlsx` and `.pptx` files, in chats, attachments and watched folders, with headings, lists, tables, every sheet and speaker notes kept. It can also write to spreadsheets, fill a Word template's `{{placeholders}}`, and create slide decks. Each change asks first, and **Undo this run** puts it back.
- **Pages, Numbers and Keynote.** Vireo reads iWork files by asking the app to export a temporary copy, which is deleted straight after. An app it opens for this is closed again, and documents you have open aren't changed. You can also fill a Pages template.
- **Decks that open in Keynote.** Decks Vireo creates now open in Keynote as well as PowerPoint.
- **Better previews.** Word, Excel and PowerPoint files have a **Contents** view (formulas, every slide and its notes) and a **Page** view. The preview updates when the file changes, including after Undo. You can resize the pane, and it keeps its width.
- **Safer saving.** Saving a new document over an existing file always asks, even if you chose **Don't ask again**. A workbook with charts, pictures, pivot tables or macros is left alone rather than saved without them.

### A better assistant for longer jobs

- **A visible plan.** For longer jobs, Vireo keeps a short checklist and shows the step it's on ("Step 2 of 4") above the reply, then folds it into "Planned 4 steps, used 6 tools".
- **Check its work.** Turn on **Settings › Assistant › Check its work before answering**, and after a reply that wrote files, changed the calendar or ran a command, Vireo looks over what it did and fixes mistakes before you see the answer.
- **Exact arithmetic.** A new calculator gives exact totals instead of the model's guesses, several sums at a time.
- **Searches your earlier chats.** Ask "what did we decide about the venue last week?" and Vireo finds the chat.
- **Learns skills from what it does.** After a long job, Vireo may offer to save how it did it as a skill, or to fix a skill that went wrong. Offers wait in **Window › Assistant › Approvals**, and nothing is saved until you approve.
- **Notices things worth remembering.** When you mention a lasting fact about yourself, or the people you work with and their roles, Vireo offers to remember it. Only your own messages are read for this. Turn it on or off in **Settings › Memory**.
- **Remembers long chats.** When a chat grows past what the model can see, the earlier part is summarized, so long chats keep their thread.
- **Runs your Shortcuts.** Ask Vireo to run one of your Shortcuts, with text if you like. It asks before each run.
- **Tasks that do more.** Scheduled tasks can now use a chat's full set of tools, at the folder access you give them, for up to an hour, and their results have **Undo this run**. A task can also start when new files arrive in a folder, or when its private link is opened.
- **Tuned for each model.** Gemma 4, Qwen and Muse Glimmer each get their own settings and instructions. The new **Recommended** reply style follows them.
- **Built for your files.** Vireo keeps track of the project folder it's working in and reads the files you name before it starts. It reads the policy for money, travel and donation tasks, gives totals, and marks things as waiting for approval until they've been approved. It flags claims of approval it finds in files and emails as unverified.

### Faster

- **Fewer tools per step.** Each step offers only the tools the request needs, so the model reads about a quarter as much before a plain question.
- **Less re-reading.** Vireo keeps the start of the prompt the same for the whole job and caches more of it, so the model skips what it has already read. On one test task, model time dropped from 83 to 51 seconds.
- **Less thinking on routine steps.** Saving a file or filling a form no longer gets the same long think as planning the job. When Calendar is off, Vireo says so at once instead of searching for minutes.
- **Smarter runs.** Thinking carries over between steps. A job ends only after three repeats in a row, and if it's cut off, it says what it changed.

### A simpler Settings

- **Its own window.** Settings is a proper macOS window (⌘,), with pages down the left in Vireo's colours. Every change is saved as you make it, and a **Restart Now** bar appears when a change needs the server to restart.
- **Clearer pages.** New **Chats** and **Connections** pages, with Calendar, web search and MCP servers in one place. The **Model** page keeps the everyday choices: the model, Start and Stop, and reasoning. Everything technical (runtime, cache, draft models, ports, folders, logs and diagnostics) is on the new **Advanced** page.
- **Easier choices.** On the **Assistant** page, the answer style is a menu and "Change files without asking" is a switch. Tools are grouped (Web, Files, Calendar and so on) with plain names. Search keys and the briefing's city save as you type.
- **One Start button.** If the model isn't running, pages that need it show a single bar with **Start**.

### Also new

- **Start the model from the welcome screen.** It shows which model is selected and whether it's loaded, with a **Start Model** button.
- **Assistant in the sidebar.** An Assistant button sits above Documents, with a badge counting approvals waiting for you. When some are waiting, it opens straight to them.
- **Chat Markdown.** Horizontal lines and `- [ ]` checklists are drawn properly, and tables have wider columns.

### Fixes

- The chat now scrolls far enough to show **Undo this run** after a reply ends.
- A step that failed no longer shows as done. It's marked "failed" in red.
- Memo headers (To, From, Date, Re) in Word and PDF documents keep their own lines, and PDF table columns no longer break words.
- A template saved with a bare file name goes beside the template, not in your home folder.
- Relative paths are worked out from the chat's project folder, and folders are no longer created in your home folder by mistake.
- Searching files looks inside PDFs and Word documents too.
- Whole spreadsheets are read when they're small, and Vireo says plainly when rows are left out.
- Links from search results are kept exactly as they were, instead of being retyped.
- With Calendar or Reminders off, Vireo no longer tries to reach them another way.
- Approvals that time out are no longer asked again, and repeated skill offers don't queue twice.
- A stopped reply no longer keeps its unfinished thinking.
- When the server's Python is incomplete, the Start banner says so.
- The Window menu lists each window once.
- Reading two Numbers or Keynote files at once no longer interrupts one of the exports.
- Large workbooks open faster and use about a third of the memory.

### Under the hood

- MLX 0.32.4, mlx-vlm 0.7.12, mlx-lm 0.32.2, llama.cpp b12228, Python 3.11.18 and Sparkle 2.10.1, plus the latest releases of the other bundled packages.
- Two unused packages were removed from the bundled Python.
- The GGUF context length now grows with your Mac's memory.

### Windows

This is a Mac-only release. The **new native Windows app** is coming soon. Until then, keep using the current Windows app (0.7.5) from the [0.7.7 release](https://github.com/InvurseStudio/Vireo-Releases/releases/tag/v0.7.7). It works with Vireo 0.9 on your Mac.

### Requirements

- Apple Silicon (M1 or later), macOS 14 or later. Apple Intelligence features need macOS 26 or later.
